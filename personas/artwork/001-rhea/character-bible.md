# Rhea (001) - Scene Illustration Identity Lock v1

## Canonical Reference

- `../../portraits/001.png`: authority for face, hair silhouette, proportions,
  outfit construction, accessories and colours.
- The existing Chinese persona is a concept draft, not confirmed canon.

## Observed Signals

- Pale ash-blonde twin ponytails, straight fringe, long side strands.
- Grey eyes, fair skin, slim anime proportions and a restrained neutral face.
- Black leather jacket worn low at the shoulders, long buckled sleeves.
- White fitted ribbed high-neck sleeveless top, black choker and cross pendant.
- High-waisted black trousers, belt, silver waist chains and black platform boots.

## First-Glance Promise

A self-directed city explorer who knows an unexpected route and invites the
viewer along without taking their choice away.

## Five Identity Pillars

| Pillar | Draft Choice | Visible Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role | Urban route planner | A folded route map and a deliberate walking direction |
| Emotional temperature | Reserved warmth | Small, relaxed smile; no exaggerated grin |
| Social energy | Direct, selectively welcoming | Looking back to invite, not pulling the viewer |
| Rhythm | Decisive but attentive | Natural step, stable balance, unhurried gesture |
| Contradiction | Independent yet makes room for others | An open invitation and enough space to follow |

The personality cues above are creative interpretation of the draft, not facts
inferred from appearance. Confidence in visible wardrobe/hair locks is high;
the proposed emotional reading is medium and remains open to user revision.

## Expression Grammar

- Baseline: calm eyes, level chin, relaxed mouth.
- Invitation: slight smile, attentive gaze back toward the viewer.
- Focus: clear gaze and purposeful movement, not an angry scowl.
- Never read as: a different character, a pin-up pose, or a helpless passenger.

## Hard Locks

Preserve both ponytails, ash-blonde colour, fringe, face geometry, grey eyes,
slim proportions, complete black-and-white outfit, choker, cross, waist chains
and boots. Do not add armour, ears, wings, new tattoos or new jewellery.

## Controlled Variables

Pose, small expression changes, wind movement, perspective and scene lighting
may change for this illustration. A paper route map is a story prop, not a new
permanent model accessory. The original model and reference remain untouched.

## Scene Direction

One portrait-format, full-body illustration in a lived-in city side street at
late afternoon. Rhea is taking a step into a new route, looking back at the
viewer, holding an unfolded paper map in one hand and giving a small invitation
with the other. Crisp 2D anime character rendering and a carefully painted
environment; no text, logos, interface, extra foreground characters or heavy blur.

This is a scene key visual, not a neutral Live2D rig source, layered PSD or
replacement MOC3 model. No claim of rig-readiness is made.

## Prompt Lock

Same fictional character as the reference: pale ash-blonde twin ponytails with
straight fringe, grey eyes and matching face proportions; black low-shoulder
leather jacket over white ribbed high-neck top; choker, cross pendant,
high-waisted black trousers, silver waist chains, black platform boots.

Negative: identity drift, different hair, missing ponytail, different eye colour,
added fantasy anatomy, new outfit, sexualised framing, exaggerated body shape,
extra fingers, broken hands, cropped feet, text, logos, watermark, heavy blur.
